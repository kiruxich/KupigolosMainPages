# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The first deliverable is the responsive home page `index.html` without a framework or build step.

## Users

Companies, agencies, creators and private customers who need professional voice-over, dubbing, audio advertising, localization, scripts, IVR recordings or AI voice tools. Visitors need to evaluate voices and studio credibility quickly, then move to voice selection, a calculator or an order.

## Product Purpose

Kupigolos helps customers choose professional voices and order end-to-end audio production. The home page must explain the studio's services, demonstrate real voices and work, establish trust and lead visitors to an order.

## Positioning

The studio combines a large catalog of Russian and foreign voices, well-known dubbing actors, multilingual localization, professional sound production and AI voice services in one place.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare voice samples, browse service categories and completed projects, estimate costs and contact the studio. The page represents a physical recording studio in Moscow and an online service available to remote customers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the current public home page's SEO title, description, key headings, factual copy, service vocabulary and important links.
- Preserve the brand name Kupigolos and the main conversion paths: choose a voice, calculate price, listen to work and contact the studio.
- Include the complete long-form home-page structure rather than only the first few blocks.
- Provide three selectable minimalist visual concepts on the same page.
- Keep one semantic copy of the content in the DOM so the visual switcher does not duplicate SEO text.
- Make the page usable on desktop, tablet and mobile.
- AI services must use six distinct service presentations: voice generator, text-to-speech, AI voice-over, video voice-over, music generator and song generator.
- Do not invent commercial claims, customers, ratings, prices or testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the existing Kupigolos name, recognizable warm red accent, Russian-language voice and studio-focused subject matter. Existing public photos, voice portraits, audio samples, customer logos and founder photo are valid source material. The visual redesign may replace the existing layout completely.

## Product Principles

- Preserve search-relevant content and information architecture while replacing the visual system.
- Let real voices, projects and studio evidence carry trust.
- Make every conversion path obvious without turning every block into a card.
- Use restrained, purposeful motion that supports audio playback and state changes.
- Treat desktop and mobile as equally finished experiences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable navigation, theme switcher and audio controls; visible focus states; sufficient contrast; meaningful alternative text; reduced-motion support; responsive layouts without horizontal page overflow.
